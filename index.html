Daniel Adewoye is a creative director and filmmaker working at the intersection of story, culture and strategy — directing commercials, YouTube content and campaigns built to connect emotionally and perform in the real world.

With 7+ years across film, branding and digital storytelling, he's built a portfolio spanning branded content, documentaries, music videos and culture-led campaigns — formerly Head of Visuals at Glitch Africa, and founder of Lens, Light & Motion, a filmmaker education platform, and Dixtro, a production company serving clients locally and internationally.
